Instant Pot Mexican Rice

The recipe for Instant Pot Mexican Rice on Allrecipes is a straightforward and highly-rated side dish designed to be a foolproof way to make a Mexican restaurant-style rice at home . Created by user "Bren," the recipe focuses on building flavor in layers directly in the Instant Pot. It begins by using the sauté function to cook onion in avocado oil until soft, followed by minced garlic until fragrant. Long-grain rice is then added and stirred until it's lightly toasted and coated in the oil, a key step for texture and flavor .

After toasting the rice, the recipe calls for deglazing the pot with low-sodium chicken stock, ensuring any browned bits are scraped from the bottom to prevent a burn warning. The dish is finished before pressure cooking by stirring in tomato sauce, salt, ground cumin, and a pinch of cayenne pepper for a subtle warmth . The pot is then sealed and cooked on high pressure for just 7 minutes. Once the cooking cycle is complete, the pressure is released using the quick-release method. The result is a fluffy and flavorful rice that serves as the perfect accompaniment to a wide variety of Mexican meals .

Ingredient

  • 1 tablespoon avocado oil, or more as needed
  • ½ medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 large cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 1 ½ cups low-sodium chicken stock
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper

Steps for making dish

  1. Turn on a multi-functional pressure cooker (such as Instant Pot); select sauté function and adjust to medium.
  2. Cover the bottom of the pot with avocado oil. Cook and stir onion until soft, 4 to 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  3. Add rice to the pot; mix until coated with oil and lightly browned.
  4. Pour in chicken stock; stir any browned bits off the bottom of the pot.
  5. Mix in tomato sauce, salt, cumin, and cayenne pepper.
  6. Close and lock the lid. Seal the vent and select high-pressure function. Set a timer for 7 minutes; allow 10 to 15 minutes for pressure to build.
  7. Release pressure carefully using the quick-release method according to the manufacturer's instructions, about 5 minutes. Unlock and remove the lid. Stir rice before serving.
