Bloomin' Potato

The Bloomin' Potatoes recipe on Allrecipes, created by contributor Diana Moutsopoulos, is a creative and visually impressive side dish inspired by the concept of a "blooming" onion. The recipe transforms simple potatoes into a fun, shareable appetizer or side by cutting them to create a "blooming" effect before baking .

The method involves using small to medium potatoes (like Yukon Gold or red potatoes). The cook makes thin, evenly spaced crosswise slices across each potato, stopping just short of cutting all the way through the bottom, so the slices remain attached and fan out slightly. The potatoes are then arranged in a baking dish, drizzled with olive oil, and generously seasoned with salt, pepper, and dried herbs like rosemary or thyme .

During baking, the sliced potatoes fan open further, with the edges becoming crispy and golden while the centers stay tender and fluffy. The result is an elegant and rustic dish that looks much more complicated than it actually is to prepare. It's often finished with a sprinkle of fresh parsley and flaky sea salt, and can be served with a dipping sauce like sour cream, aioli, or a garlic-herb yogurt .

Ingredients

  • 4 (6 to 8 ounce) russet or Yukon gold potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted

Sauce

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

Steps in making Dish

  1. Wash and scrub potatoes thoroughly until cleaned.
  2. Using a sharp knife, slice potatoes crosswise into 1/8-inch-thick cuts, stopping when the knife reaches 1/2-inch from the bottom so the potato remains attached at the base. Rotate the potato and repeat lengthwise, making 1/8-inch-thick cuts without slicing all the way through.
  3. Place cut potatoes into a 2-quart square baking dish. Cover with vented plastic wrap. Cook in the microwave on high for 5 minutes.
  4. Combine garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Brush potatoes with melted butter and sprinkle evenly with seasoning mixture.
  5. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Line a baking pan with aluminum foil and place potatoes on the prepared pan. Bake in the preheated oven until browned and crispy, 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. Stir together mayonnaise, sour cream, ketchup, horseradish, vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl until smooth.
  7. Serve potatoes hot with dipping sauce on the side.
